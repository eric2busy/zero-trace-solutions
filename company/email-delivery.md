# Transactional Email Delivery

## Production configuration

Transactional email is sent by Resend from `Zero Trace Solutions <solutions@zerotraceusa.com>`. Replies go to `support@zerotraceusa.com`, which remains hosted by Proton Mail.

Namecheap is authoritative for `zerotraceusa.com`. The verified Resend configuration is:

| Purpose | Type | Host | Value | Priority | TTL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resend DKIM | TXT | `resend._domainkey` | Value generated by Resend | — | Automatic |
| Resend return-path | MX | `send` | `feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com` | 30 | Automatic |
| Resend return-path SPF | TXT | `send` | `v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all` | — | Automatic |

The Resend MX record applies only to `send.zerotraceusa.com`. The Proton records at the root domain remain unchanged:

- `@` MX priority 10 → `mail.protonmail.ch`
- `@` MX priority 20 → `mailsec.protonmail.ch`

Do not enable Resend receiving on the root domain. Doing so could redirect inbound mail away from Proton.

## Required Vercel environment variables

- `RESEND_API_KEY`: Resend credential; secret and never committed.
- `LEAD_ALERT_FROM`: Optional sending identity. Production currently uses `Zero Trace Solutions <solutions@zerotraceusa.com>`.
- `LEAD_ALERT_TO`: Internal notification/reply address. Defaults to `support@zerotraceusa.com`.

Changing a production secret requires explicit owner approval.

## Observability

Each attempted external email action emits a structured `transactional_email_delivery` log entry containing the endpoint, message type, recipient category, result, and Resend HTTP status when available. Customer addresses are not included in the delivery log entry.

`/api/leads` and `/api/book` return `emailDelivery` metadata. Email failure does not undo or fail an otherwise successful lead or booking. Provider rejection uses reason `provider_rejected`; transport errors use `request_failed`; missing configuration uses `resend_not_configured`.

## Production verification

1. Confirm `send.zerotraceusa.com` publicly resolves to the MX and TXT records above.
2. Confirm Resend shows `zerotraceusa.com` as verified.
3. Submit one owner-controlled walkthrough through the live website.
4. Confirm the Notion lead is created and both the customer acknowledgement and internal alert arrive.
5. Select a verified slot and finish the booking.
6. Confirm the Calendar event and Notion Scheduled state, then confirm customer and internal booking emails arrive.
7. Inspect Vercel runtime logs for successful `transactional_email_delivery` entries and no Resend 403 responses.
8. Confirm inbound mail to `support@zerotraceusa.com` still arrives in Proton.

Do not close Issue #2 until the entire production path above passes.

## Rollback

If the Resend return-path configuration causes an unexpected problem, disable transactional sending by removing or correcting `RESEND_API_KEY` only with owner approval. The application will continue to save leads and bookings and will report `resend_not_configured` in email metadata.

The Resend-specific `send` MX/TXT records may be removed only with owner approval. Never remove or replace the Proton root MX records. Reverting the observability code does not require a DNS rollback.
