FAQ

Common questions

Is this the same as cleaning?

No. We sanitize — we don’t sweep, mop, or dust unless the job requires it. We treat surfaces with an electrostatic misting system and hospital-grade solutions.

How long until the space can be used again?

Most spaces are ready to reoccupy within minutes. The mist dries invisibly and leaves no residue.

What areas do you serve?

Inland Empire & San Bernardino Valley. Contact us if you’re nearby and we’ll confirm coverage.

Can I set a recurring schedule?

Yes. Many clients book single visits or set regular maintenance so protection stays consistent.
