ABOUT

Quiet competence, on repeat.

Zero Trace Solutions provides hospital-grade sanitizing for high-traffic commercial spaces—offices, classrooms, retail floors, and client-facing environments—across the Inland Empire and San Bernardino Valley.

We are not a traditional cleaning company. We don’t sweep, mop, or dust unless a job specifically requires it. Our work is focused: treat the surfaces people actually touch, using electrostatic misting and EPA-registered solutions, then leave without a visible trace.

That difference matters. Most facilities already have cleaning covered. What they often lack is consistent, documented sanitizing of high-touch zones between deeper cleans—workstations, door hardware, shared equipment, restrooms, and lobby touchpoints—without shutting the floor down for hours.

How we work

We schedule around your operations. A walkthrough helps us map your space, confirm coverage, and recommend single visits or a recurring cadence. On service day we arrive on time, treat the agreed zones, document the visit, and clear out so your team can get back to work within minutes.

Who we’re built for

Teams that need reliable protection without the noise of a full cleaning crew: office managers, school administrators, clinic and studio operators, and commercial property contacts who want a calm, repeatable process they can plan around.
